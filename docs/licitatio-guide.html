Licitatio Bid Desk · Documentation

Everything your team needs to be bid ready.

This guide explains how to use Licitatio, from creating your workspace to exporting a complete bid packet. It is written for company owners, bid coordinators, engineers who help prepare bids, and Semivra staff.

01 / Overview

What Licitatio does

Licitatio is a bid readiness workspace for Philippine construction firms that compete for public and private projects. It keeps your eligibility documents, track record and bids in one place, warns you before anything expires, and assembles your bid packet and technical proposal.

The problem it solves

In public bidding, a strong offer can still be disqualified because of one expired permit or a missing certificate. Most firms keep documents in folders and spreadsheets and retype the same schedules for every bid. Licitatio makes the paperwork ready before the invitation to bid arrives.

The main parts

  • Document vault. Permits, licenses, tax and financial documents with versions and expiry dates.
  • Registers. Your projects (experience), key personnel and equipment.
  • Bids. Each opportunity you are considering, from first sighting to award.
  • Bid packets. The checklist of requirements for one bid, matched to your documents, with a readiness check and a single indexed PDF export.
  • Proposals. Technical proposals built from sections and reusable text, exported to Word or PDF.
  • Tasks, reminders and outcomes. Who does what, what is about to expire or fall due, and how your bids turned out.
Important

Licitatio prepares and checks your documents. It never submits a bid on your behalf. Your team always reviews every export and submits it to the procuring entity.

02 / Getting started

Getting started

Create your workspace

  1. Open the Licitatio website and choose Start free trial.
  2. Enter your name, work email, a password of at least 10 characters, your company name and company type (contractor, design and build, consultant or developer).
  3. Open the verification email and select the link. Links work once and expire after 24 hours. If yours expired, sign in and request a new one.
  4. Sign in. You are the owner of the new workspace, and your 14-day trial starts.

Your first hour

Most teams are ready for their first bid after about an hour of setup. Work in this order, because each step feeds the next.

  1. Company profile. Fill in the legal name, address and authorized signatory. Exports cannot be produced without them.
  2. Document vault. Upload the documents every bid asks for, with their issue and expiry dates.
  3. Registers. Add your completed and ongoing projects, key personnel and major equipment. You can import these from a spreadsheet.
  4. Team. Invite your colleagues and give each one the right role.
  5. First bid. Add a bid, choose a requirement template and work the packet until it shows Ready.

Joining a team

If a colleague invited you, open the invitation email and choose Accept invitation. If you already have an account, sign in; otherwise set your name and password. Invitations expire after 7 days; ask the owner or an admin to send a new one if needed.

Signing in and passwords

  • After several wrong passwords the account is locked for a short time to protect it. Wait and try again, or reset your password.
  • Forgot your password? sends a reset link that expires in 1 hour. Resetting signs you out on every device.
  • You stay signed in on a device until you sign out or your session ends.

05 / Document vault

Document vault

The vault holds every document you reuse across bids. Each document has a type, a title, its file, and optional issue and expiry dates.

Adding a document

  1. Open Document vault and choose Add document.
  2. Pick the document type, such as Mayor's or business permit, PCAB contractor's license or Tax clearance certificate.
  3. Upload the file. PDF, PNG, JPG and Word (DOCX) files up to 15 MB are accepted.
  4. Enter the issue and expiry dates exactly as printed. Reminders depend on the expiry date being right.

Reading details from a scan

When AI features are turned on for your workspace, choose the file and then Read details from the scan. Licitatio reads the document type, reference number, issue date and expiry date from a PDF or photo and fills the form. Always check every detail against the paper before saving: a blurry photo can be misread, and anything Licitatio is unsure of is left blank. The scan is sent to our AI provider only when you press the button.

Document types included

The vault comes with the documents most invitations to bid ask for, grouped by category: PhilGEPS registration certificate, DTI business name, SEC or CDA registration, Mayor's or business permit, PCAB contractor's license, BIR certificate of registration, tax clearance, income tax return, audited financial statements, NFCC computation, organizational chart, and more.

Status of a document

Expired past its expiry date. Expiring soon inside the reminder window. Valid current. No expiry documents such as an SEC registration.

Renewing a document

When you receive a renewed permit, open the document and upload a new version instead of adding a new document. Older versions stay in the history, and every bid packet that uses the document picks up the current version automatically.

Archiving

Archive documents you no longer use. Archived documents leave the main list and stop sending reminders, but stay available under the archived filter.

Importing from a spreadsheet

  1. On any register, choose Import and download the template.
  2. Fill it in a spreadsheet program and save it as CSV. Up to 500 rows per import.
  3. Upload the file. Licitatio shows a preview with any problems row by row before anything is saved.
  4. Fix the flagged rows or continue with the valid ones, then confirm.

Record PRC license expiry dates for key personnel. The personnel list shows which licenses have lapsed or are close to lapsing, so you can renew them before they appear in a bid.

07 / Bids

Bids

A bid is one opportunity: a project you have seen on PhilGEPS, received from a private client, or heard about. Add the title, procuring entity, reference number, location, approved budget for the contract and the submission deadline.

Stages of a bid

Identified→Evaluating→Preparing→Ready→Submitted→Opened→Awarded or Lost

A bid can also end as No bid (you decided not to bid) or Cancelled (the procuring entity cancelled it). The rules that keep the tracker honest:

  • To move from Evaluating to Preparing, record a go decision first.
  • A bid can only move to Ready when its packet has no blockers.
  • No bid, Cancelled and Lost need a short reason, so the team remembers why.
  • When you mark a bid Awarded or Lost, you can record your bid amount, the winning amount, the winner and whether you were disqualified. These feed the Outcomes page.
  • A bid in Preparing or Ready can step back one stage if plans change.

Board and table views

The board shows bids as cards in columns by stage. The table view suits long lists and sorting. Countdown chips show how many days are left before the deadline.

Inside a bid

Each bid has tabs for Overview, Packet, Proposal, Tasks and Activity. Activity records every change: who moved the bid, who changed the packet, and when.

08 / Bid packets

Bid packets

The packet is the checklist of everything one bid requires, grouped into eligibility, technical, financial, post-qualification and other documents. Licitatio checks every item and tells you whether the packet is ready.

Creating a packet

  1. Open the bid, go to Packet and choose a requirement template.
  2. Licitatio matches each requirement to a current document in your vault.
  3. Work through the items that are not yet complete until the summary says Ready.

Requirement templates

  • Public bidding: infrastructure (default for government works)
  • Private bid: basic
  • Small value procurement, Public bidding: design and build and Consulting services, marked as drafts while our engineer reviews them

Admins can copy a template and adjust it, or add a requirement to a single packet. Always compare the template with the actual invitation to bid: requirements change between agencies and projects.

How each item is fulfilled

TypeMeaning
From the vaultUses a document from your vault. It must still be valid on the submission date, with any minimum validity the template asks for.
UploadA file made for this bid only, such as a signed bid form or a bid security.
GeneratedA schedule Licitatio builds from your records: completed projects, ongoing projects, key personnel, equipment, or the NFCC computation.
ExternalSomething handled outside the app, such as a notarized affidavit that is still at the notary.

Item status

Complete Expiring Expired Missing Waived Pending outside

A document that expires before the submission date counts as expired for that bid, even if it is valid today. Mandatory items that are missing or expired are blockers.

Item actions

  • Change picks a different vault document or file.
  • Unassign removes the current document from the item.
  • Waive marks an optional item as not needed for this bid, with a reason. Mandatory items cannot be waived by Semivra staff.
  • Exclude removes an item that does not apply to this bid.
  • Mark pending outside for items being handled outside the app, such as at the notary.
  • Notes keep remarks like "original at the office".

Financial capacity (NFCC) and bid security

Public infrastructure bids ask for your Net Financial Contracting Capacity. Licitatio computes it on the bid's Overview as soon as the bid has an approved budget and your company profile has its financial figures:

Formula

NFCC = (current assets minus current liabilities) x 15, minus the outstanding works on your ongoing contracts and awarded contracts not yet started.

  1. In Settings, Company profile, fill Financial figures from your latest audited financial statements.
  2. In Experience, give each ongoing project its percent complete. A project without one counts at its full contract amount.
  3. Open the bid. The Financial capacity card shows your NFCC, whether it covers the approved budget, any shortfall, and the bid security to prepare: 2% of the budget in cash or a bank guarantee, or 5% as a surety bond.
  4. In the packet, generate the NFCC computation item to print the form with your figures.

The factor of 15 and the 2% and 5% rates are the common defaults. Always confirm them against the bid documents of each project.

Submission checklist and envelope labels

On submission day, choose Submission checklist in the packet. Tick each item as it goes into the envelope, set whether it is an original, a certified true copy or a photocopy, and mark notarized papers. It works on a phone, so you can tick items off at the office or at the BAC.

Print checklist and envelope labels makes a PDF with a label for the first envelope (eligibility and technical documents) and the second envelope (financial proposal), each with the contract name, reference number, procuring entity, your company and the opening date, followed by the packing checklist. Packing progress never clears a client approval. Follow any marking rules in the bid documents; they take precedence.

Client approval

If Require approval before export is turned on in settings, an owner or admin must approve the packet before a final export. Any later change to the packet clears the approval. Without approval, exports are marked "DRAFT: awaiting client approval".

Exporting

Choose Export PDF. Licitatio merges every document into one PDF with a cover and an index of items and page numbers. Large packets take a minute; you get a notification when the export is ready, and the download stays available for 30 days. A packet with blockers can only be exported as a marked draft.

09 / Proposals

Proposals

The proposal builder writes technical proposals: company background, method statement, work plan, quality and safety approach, and any section the bid asks for. Proposals are available on the Pro and Desk plans.

Building a proposal

  • Create a proposal and link it to a bid so bid details fill in automatically.
  • Add sections and fill them with paragraphs, headings, lists, tables and images.
  • Use Insert block to copy saved text from the content library, such as your standard safety policy. Inserted text is a copy, so each proposal stays editable on its own.

Placeholders

Placeholders are filled from your profile, registers and the linked bid when you export. The side panel lists every placeholder and warns about any that have no value yet.

Draft with AI

When AI drafting is turned on for your workspace, each section has a Draft with AI button. Choose company background, method statement and work plan, quality and safety approach, or describe another section, and add instructions if you like.

  • The draft uses your company profile, registers and linked bid. Your TIN and contact details are never sent.
  • Nothing is inserted until you review the draft and choose Insert into section.
  • Drafts can be wrong. Read every sentence and check every fact before you submit.
  • A workspace can request up to 50 drafts per day.

Exporting a proposal

Export to Word (DOCX) for final edits, or PDF for submission. If placeholders are still empty, Licitatio asks before exporting with blanks.

10 / Tasks

Tasks

Tasks track the work that has to happen outside the app: signing forms, notarizing, getting the bid security, delivering originals. Each task has a title, an assignee, a due date and an optional link to a bid.

Tasks move through To do, Doing, Blocked and Done on the task board. Assignees get a notification when a task is given to them, and the dashboard shows each member's open tasks.

12 / Outcomes

Outcomes

The Outcomes page turns your bid history into lessons. It shows:

  • Win rate across decided bids (awarded compared with awarded plus lost).
  • Total value of awarded bids.
  • How often bids were disqualified, and for what.
  • The most common reasons for losing and for deciding not to bid.
  • The packet requirements that most often blocked a bid.
  • The median number of days from adding a bid to submitting it.
  • Counts of bids by stage, for any date range you choose.

The numbers are only as good as the outcomes you record, so enter the winning amount and winner whenever results are posted.

14 / Semivra desk service

Semivra desk service

On the Desk plan, Semivra staff help prepare your bids inside your workspace. On Pro you can also grant staff access for help and support.

  • The owner grants access under Settings, Staff access, naming the staff member and the number of days, from 1 to 90 (30 by default).
  • The owner can revoke access at any time, and it ends on its own when the period runs out.
  • A yellow banner reminds staff they are working in a client workspace, and every action they take is recorded in your audit log as Semivra staff.
  • Staff sign in with two-factor authentication.
  • Staff cannot waive mandatory requirements, approve packets, manage members, staff access or plans, or read the audit log.

16 / Security and privacy

Security and privacy

  • Separation. Each company's data is kept apart from every other company's.
  • Encryption. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Staff two-factor secrets get an extra layer of encryption.
  • Passwords. Stored only as a secure hash. Nobody at Semivra can see your password.
  • Downloads. File links are short-lived and work only for signed-in members.
  • Audit log. Owners and admins can see who did what and when under Settings, Audit log.
  • Backups. Encrypted backups run nightly and are kept for 30 days.

17 / For Semivra staff

For Semivra staff

Onboarding a client

Plan about 60 minutes with the client's owner. Before you start, ask how long their last packet took to assemble; the goal is under 30 minutes for the next one.

  1. The owner registers and verifies their email. A platform admin sets the plan after payment is recorded.
  2. The owner grants you staff access (default 30 days).
  3. Complete the company profile: legal name, address and signatory first.
  4. Upload the core documents with careful expiry dates.
  5. Enter projects, key personnel with PRC license expiry, and equipment.
  6. Create the first bid, record the go decision, choose a template and work the packet to Ready.
  7. Create tasks for what only the client can do, such as signing and notarizing, and assign them to the client's members.
  8. Export the packet. The client reviews it and submits the bid themselves.

Platform administration

  • Platform admins see all organizations on the admin page and can change a plan and its expiry date, for example after a bank transfer.
  • Staff accounts, authenticator resets, client data exports and deletion requests are handled by the operations team with the support tools described in the operations runbook. Deletions need a written request from the owner.

18 / Troubleshooting and FAQ

Troubleshooting and FAQ

I did not receive the verification or reset email.

Check spam and promotions folders, then request a new link from the sign-in page. Verification links expire after 24 hours and reset links after 1 hour.

My bid will not move to Ready.

The packet still has blockers. Open the Packet tab: the summary shows how many mandatory items are missing or expired. A document that expires before the submission date counts as expired.

I cannot move a bid from Evaluating to Preparing.

Record a go decision on the bid's Overview tab first.

Export says the company profile is incomplete.

Fill in the legal name, street address, city, province, and the signatory's name and position under Settings, Company profile.

My export is marked DRAFT.

Either the packet still has blockers, or approval before export is required and an owner or admin has not approved it yet, or the packet changed after approval.

Upload fails.

Only PDF, PNG, JPG and DOCX files up to 15 MB are accepted, and your plan's storage must have room. Scan large documents at a lower resolution or split them.

The workspace is read-only.

The plan expired more than 7 days ago. The owner can renew under Settings, Plan. Everything is still there and can be downloaded.

I do not see Draft with AI.

AI drafting must be turned on for the workspace, the plan must include proposals, and you need the editor role or higher.

I am not getting SMS reminders.

Add a Philippine mobile number and turn on SMS reminders under Settings, Notifications, Your reminders. SMS covers only the most urgent items.

Can Licitatio submit the bid for us?

No. Your team reviews and submits every bid. Licitatio prepares and checks the documents.

19 / Glossary

Glossary

ABC
Approved budget for the contract: the ceiling price set by the procuring entity.
Blocker
A mandatory packet item that is missing or expired. A bid cannot be Ready while it has blockers.
Go decision
The recorded choice to pursue a bid, needed before preparing it.
NFCC
Net financial contracting capacity, a financial eligibility requirement in public bidding.
PCAB
Philippine Contractors Accreditation Board, which licenses contractors and sets categories from AAAA to Trade/E.
PhilGEPS
Philippine Government Electronic Procurement System, where public bids are posted and suppliers register.
Post-qualification
The procuring entity's check of the lowest bidder's documents after bid opening.
Procuring entity
The agency or company that runs the bidding.
RA 9184
The Government Procurement Reform Act and its implementing rules.
Requirement template
A standard list of packet requirements that Licitatio uses to start a packet.
Vault
Licitatio's store of reusable company documents.
