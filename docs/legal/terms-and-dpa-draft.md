## Terms of service: points to cover

1. **Parties and acceptance.** [Legal entity] and the subscribing company; who may accept on a company's behalf.
2. **The service.** A bid readiness workspace: document vault with expiry reminders, registers, bid tracker, bid
   packets with a readiness verdict, proposal builder, exports. **Semivra does not submit bids**; the Client reviews
   every export and submits it.
3. **No legal or procurement advice.** Requirement templates are defaults reviewed by our civil engineer but must be
   checked against the actual invitation to bid and current rules (RA 9184 and its IRR, or the [applicable law at
   launch]). The readiness verdict reflects the data entered; it is not a guarantee of eligibility.
4. **Plans and payment.** Starter, Pro and Desk; prices [PHP]; billed [monthly/annually] by [bank transfer, GCash,
   invoice]; 14-day trial; 7-day grace period after expiry; then read-only, with reading and downloads still allowed.
5. **Desk service (Desk plan).** Bid preparation by Semivra staff [up to 2 bids per month]; staff access is granted and
   revocable by the owner, time-boxed (max 90 days), audited; staff cannot waive mandatory requirements, manage
   members, staff access, plans or the audit log.
6. **Client responsibilities.** Accurate data; lawful basis for personnel data entered; keeping originals; account
   security; not uploading unlawful content.
7. **Availability and support.** [Target availability, maintenance windows, support hours and channel.]
8. **Data ownership and export.** The Client owns its data; on request we provide a full export (JSON and files).
9. **Termination.** By either party with [notice]; data retained [N] days then deleted, earlier on written request.
10. **Liability.** Cap at [fees paid in the prior 12 months]; exclusion of indirect damages; specific carve-out for
    disqualification or lost contracts arising from bid content [counsel to advise].
11. **Governing law and venue.** Republic of the Philippines; courts of [city].

## Data processing clause: points to cover

- **Roles:** Client is personal information controller; Semivra is processor for Client-entered personal data.
- **Instructions:** process only to provide the service and on documented Client instructions.
- **Confidentiality:** personnel with access (desk staff, engineers) are bound by confidentiality.
- **Security measures:** as in the privacy notice: encryption, isolation, access control, TOTP for staff, audit log,
  backups, short-lived download links.
- **Sub-processors:** the list in the privacy notice; [notice period] before adding one; Client may object.
- **Cross-border transfer:** Singapore hosting [and others]; safeguards.
- **Data subject requests:** Semivra assists the Client within [N] days.
- **Breach notification:** Semivra notifies the Client without undue delay and within [24–48] hours of discovery,
  so the Client can meet the 72-hour NPC requirement.
- **Return and deletion:** export on request; deletion within [N] days of termination or written request
  (operational tool: `npm run purge-org`).
- **Audit:** Client may request evidence of compliance [once a year, on notice].
